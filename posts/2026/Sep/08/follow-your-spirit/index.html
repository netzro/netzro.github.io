Follow Your Spirit — Always the First Thought

note-to-self:26-05

That very first feeling. That quiet, instant pulse before the mind starts negotiating — that's your spirit talking.

Then comes the second thought. The one that weighs risks, counts costs, remembers past failures. That's your ego protecting you from everything, including your own potential.

Most people live in the second thought. They rationalize their way out of every impulse, every gut nudge, every creative spark. They overthink until the signal is gone.

But your first feeling? That's not random. That's your spirit — deeper than logic, older than fear, smarter than your conscious mind trying to keep you safe.

When you feel like calling someone — do it. When you want to write something — start. When your gut says "this is the one" — trust it. When you feel called to speak — speak.

The second thought will tell you why you shouldn't. And 99% of the time, it'll be wrong.

Your spirit doesn't stutter, doesn't overthink, doesn't second-guess. It just knows.

So listen to it. Follow it. Stop consulting committees for answers your soul already gave you.

The first feeling is the truth. The second is doubt wearing a reasonable mask.

Choose the truth. Every. Single. Time.
