A Parent's Blessing

  • Sun 21 June 2026
  • misc

  • #nts, #blessing, #parenting, #love

note-to-self:26-03

You have my blessing now and at any time in your life. I trust you and I know that you are on the right path. At times I will set clear boundaries and abide by them. When you fall, I will help you up. I will protect you until you can protect yourself. I give you permission to be like me, to be more than me or to be less than me, just as your vision demands. In being just the way you are you ceaselessly bless my life. I am so proud of you. I love you.
