Make Your Own Bible

note-to-self:24-04

Make your own Bible. Select and collect all the words and sentences that in all your readings have been to you like the blast of a trumpet. — Ralph Waldo Emerson

Emerson's wisdom invites us to create a personal "Bible" — not a new holy book, but a curated collection of quotes, phrases, and ideas that profoundly shape our lives. The words that resonate like the blast of a trumpet: loud, clear, and unforgettable.

What words or phrases have struck you like a trumpet's blast? Which passages have shaped your values, beliefs, or understanding of yourself and the world?

Peace, Love, Balance. ☮️ 💚 ☯️
