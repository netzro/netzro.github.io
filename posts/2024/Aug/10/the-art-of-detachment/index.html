The Art of Detachment

note-to-self:24-02

Detachment is the ability to maintain emotional and psychological distance from situations, outcomes, and people without becoming overly involved or affected. It doesn't mean indifference or a lack of empathy — it's about not being overly influenced by others' emotions or expectations. 🙏🤍
