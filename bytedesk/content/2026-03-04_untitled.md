### Performance Metrics

Key performance indicators (KPIs) of the NGX market include:

* **Nigerian Stock Exchange All-Share Index (ASI)**: A measure of the overall NGX market performance, calculated by combining the stock prices of the 500 listed companies.
* **Market Capitalization**: The total value of all listed companies, calculated by multiplying the number of outstanding shares by the current market price.
* **Trading Volume**: The total number of shares traded on the NGX market, representing the liquidity and activity of the market.
