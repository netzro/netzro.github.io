Marvel Cinematic Universe - Phase 1

Following my commitment to watching the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) films in release order, as outlined in my previous post, I’ve completed Phase 1. This phase includes Iron Man (May 2008), The Incredible Hulk (June 2008), Iron Man 2 (April 2010), Thor (April 2011), Captain America: The First Avenger (July 2011), and The Avengers (April 2012).

Rewatching The Avengers was particularly rewarding. Viewing these films again has deepened my appreciation for the storyline, events, and character development.

I’m excited to continue with Phase 2 of the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
