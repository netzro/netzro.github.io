# Reading List
_Updated as I finish books. Last update: June 2026._

## Power & Strategy

- **48 Laws of Power** — Robert Greene. A manual on power dynamics. Some laws are ruthless, all are observational. Worth reading to recognize the game, not necessarily to play it.
- **The Richest Man in Babylon** — George S. Clason. Financial wisdom wrapped in parables set in ancient Babylon. Pay yourself first, live below your means, invest wisely. Simple principles that compound.

## Wealth & Manifestation

- **Magickal Cash Book** — Jason Miller. A practical guide to using ritual and magick for financial results. Blends the esoteric with the pragmatic — set intentions, take action, repeat.

## Philosophy & Esoteric

- **The Kybalion** — Three Initiates. The foundational text of Hermeticism. Seven principles including Mentalism, Correspondence, and Vibration. Dense but rewarding on repeated reads.
- **Our Ultimate Reality** — Adrian P. Cooper. An exploration of the nature of existence and consciousness. Challenges materialist assumptions and opens space for deeper inquiry.

## Technology

- **Bytes of Python** — Swaroop C H. A clean, no-nonsense introduction to Python. Good for beginners, useful as a quick reference for fundamentals.
