Title: Marvel Cinematic Universe - Phase 1
Date: 2024-10-01
Tags: 100daystooffload, movies
Summary: Reflections on watching Phase 1 of the Marvel Cinematic Universe films in release order.

Following my commitment to watching the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) films in release order, as outlined in my [previous post]({filename}/posts/mcu_movies.md), I’ve completed Phase 1. This phase includes *Iron Man* (May 2008), *The Incredible Hulk* (June 2008), *Iron Man 2* (April 2010), *Thor* (April 2011), *Captain America: The First Avenger* (July 2011), and *The Avengers* (April 2012).

Rewatching *The Avengers* was particularly rewarding. Viewing these films again has deepened my appreciation for the storyline, events, and character development.

I’m excited to continue with Phase 2 of the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
