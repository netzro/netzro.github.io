# The Life You Want Requires the Discipline You've Been Avoiding

The life you want is on the other side of the thing you're avoiding.

You already know what it is. You don't need another book, another video, another plan. You need to close the app, sit in the discomfort, and start — not tomorrow, not when you feel ready.

Right now.

Discipline isn't dramatic. It's just doing the thing you already know you should do, at the moment you least want to do it. Again and again, until the life you want stops being a fantasy and starts being a Tuesday.

Stop waiting. Start.
