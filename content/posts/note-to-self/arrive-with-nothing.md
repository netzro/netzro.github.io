### note-to-self:24-08

The stark reality of human existence is that we arrive with nothing material and leave with nothing material. This truth highlights the futility of misplaced priorities like greed, hatred, war, envy, and backstabbing. Instead, our focus should be on humanity, beauty, creativity, and unity — qualities that unveil the divinity within us.

Embracing these values liberates us from our vanities and establishes a noble legacy. This shift is not only possible but achievable, for we are the world.

Here's to a glorious and fulfilling existence for all, and profound peace to the world.

**Peace, Love, Balance.**
