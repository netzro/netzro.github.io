> Make your own Bible. Select and collect all the words and sentences that in all your readings have been to you like the blast of a trumpet.  
> — Ralph Waldo Emerson

Emerson’s wisdom invites us to create a personal “Bible”—not a new holy book or a replacement for sacred texts, but a curated collection of quotes, phrases, and ideas that profoundly shape our lives. These are the words that resonate like the blast of a trumpet: loud, clear, and unforgettable.

This personal guidebook draws from diverse sources—sacred texts, philosophical works, poetry, or even lived experiences. The key is to gather what speaks to our souls, sparks our imagination, and inspires us to embody compassion, wisdom, and justice.

What words or phrases have struck you like a trumpet’s blast? Which passages have shaped your values, beliefs, or understanding of yourself and the world?

**Peace, Love, Balance.**
