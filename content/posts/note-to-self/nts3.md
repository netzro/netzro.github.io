> I recognize the divine in every aspect of the natural world and honor its sacredness. I am humbled by the realization that Nature is the source of my existence, providing everything I need to live. I pay reverence to Nature, acknowledging its power and wisdom.

This statement reflects:

1. **Interconnectedness**: Recognizing the divine in all aspects of Nature.
2. **Reverence**: Honoring Nature as a sacred entity.
3. **Gratitude**: Acknowledging Nature’s role in sustaining life.

**Peace, Love, Balance.**
