Date: 2024-08-22
Tags: 100DaysToOffload, nts
Summary: A reflection on individual paths to growth, inspired by a Dostoevsky quote.

> To go wrong in one's own way is better than to go right in someone else's.  
> — Fyodor Dostoevsky

Every soul requires unique experiences for the evolution of its consciousness. What may appear as a dark or destructive path might be the exact experience a soul needs to awaken.

No one can precisely know what experiences another soul requires for its awakening. What is the right path for one may be detrimental to another.

**Peace, Love, Balance.**
